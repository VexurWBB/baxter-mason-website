Why use a buyers agent

10 reasons to use a buyers agent.

Buy the right property, at the right price, with less stress and more confidence.

Why it matters

What a buyers agent does for you.

From the first search to the day you get the keys, here is how we make buying easier.

01

Save time and skip the stress

Searching, researching, inspecting and negotiating can take months of nights and weekends. We do the heavy lifting, so you can get on with your work, your family and your life.

02

Gain more than a property

The right property, in the right location, bought with a clear strategy for your goals. For many buyers, it's the first step towards long-term financial freedom.

03

Pay what it's worth, not what the seller wants

Selling agents set price expectations to suit their seller. We base the price on real comparable sales and market evidence, so you pay what the property is actually worth.

04

Get ahead in a tight market

When good properties are scarce, waiting for the next open home isn't enough. We know what's coming up before it hits the portals, including off-market and pre-market opportunities.

05

Put the power back in your hands

We negotiate property purchases every week. We know when to hold firm and when to walk away, so you negotiate from strength, at private treaty or at auction.

06

Have someone in your corner

The selling agent works for the seller and is paid to get the highest price. Your buyers agent works only for you, from start to finish.

07

Be supported every step of the way

Buying property can feel overwhelming, especially if you're doing it on your own. We're with you through every decision, every call and every document, explained in plain language.

08

Old-school service, the way it should be

We don't just send you links. We do the inspections, check out the street and drive you to the properties worth seeing.

09

Avoid costly mistakes

We look for the red flags most buyers miss, like overpricing, flood risk, zoning issues, building defects and locations that won't grow.

10

Tap into local knowledge and networks

We know the Sunshine Coast suburb by suburb. Our relationships with local agents, building inspectors, solicitors and brokers open doors buyers often can't open on their own.

A selling agent works for the vendor. A buyer's agent works for you. At Baxter & Mason, that means honest advice, strong negotiation, and no mixed loyalties.
